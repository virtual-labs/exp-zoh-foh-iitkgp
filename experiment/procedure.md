### Procedure

<b>Steps to perform the simulation</b>

										
<ol type="1">

<li>At first enter the desired values of minimum frequency, maximum frequency and sampling time. Here default values are 1 for minimum frequency,
100 for maximum frequency and 0.1 for sampling frequency.</li></br>
<div align="center">
<img class="img-fluid"  src="./images/DV.png" alt=""><br> 
<b> Fig.1. Maximum, Minimum Frequency and Sampling time values entry </b>						  
</div><br/>

<li>Click on 'Select method for linear scale' dropdown-menu and select the desired option.
<div align="center">
<img class="img-fluid"  src="./images/Linear_Scale.png" alt=""><br> 
<b> Fig.2. Selecting method for linear scale simulation </b>							  
</div><br/>


<li>Click on 'Select method for semilog scale' dropdown-menu and select the desired option.
<div align="center">
<img class="img-fluid"  src="./images/Semilog_Scale.png" alt=""><br> 
<b> Fig.3. Selecting method for semilog simulation </b>	
</div><br/>

<li>Click on 'Plot' button for simulation. </li><br/>

<li>Click on the 'Download Plot' button to download the simulation results.</li><br/>	

<li>Click on 'Clear' button before every simulation to clear the previous plot.</li><br/> 

<li>To compare plots, select the desired options from the dropdown-menu without using the 'Clear' button.</li><br/>

</ol>
